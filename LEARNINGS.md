# LEARNINGS

Self-improvement log. After every clean run, SUPERAGENT appends:

1. A dated lesson — what worked, what broke, what to do differently next time.
2. One line: `INTENT: <what was asked> | SHIPPED: <what was delivered> | MATCH: Y/N`

At the start of every goal, SUPERAGENT reads this file (plus the last few
`INTENT | SHIPPED | MATCH` lines) and applies the lessons.
